# JagerLeagueBot
Since we're down 1 person, we use a 'bot' to fill in the gap. However, our bot only drafts the lowest possible ADP possible so that their weekly score is only updated in the even that the player they are facing does not beeat the median score of the rest of the league. 

Part 1: Auto-Calculate the median score, excluding the bot and the player that they are facing that week. 

Part 2: Generate a fun recap of the week's matchups taking into account the bot's results as well.
